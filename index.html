    Notation the parser accepts

    • Equations use one = sign: 2x + 3 = 11. Two roots are joined by or: x = 7 or x = -7.
    • Powers use ^ or superscripts: x^2 and x² are the same. Exponent 1/2 becomes a square root.
    • Roots and absolute value: sqrt(2x + 3), the radical sign with a number or bracketed group, and bars: |x - 3|.
    • Fractions use /: x/2. Decimals are exact: 0.5 is kept as 1/2.
    • Finishing: when an equation has no real solution, write no solution.
