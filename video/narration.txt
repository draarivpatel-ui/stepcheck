StepCheck is a deterministic algebra proof workbench that runs locally in your browser with zero dependencies and zero grading servers. Instead of only marking a final answer right or wrong, StepCheck verifies every algebraic step you write from the given equation down to the target. Under the hood, a recursive descent parser and symbolic equivalence engine canonicalize both sides using exact BigInt rationals, multivariate polynomials, and radical terms. Each valid move is cited in the margin by the exact rule that licenses it, such as the Addition Property, Distributive Property, or Square Root Rule. When a step is invalid, StepCheck explains why and computes a concrete counterexample showing which root was lost or warns about extraneous solutions. The workbench ships with free practice, fifteen graded challenges across three tiers ending in a seven step symbolic derivation of the quadratic formula, and a thirteen entry rule book whose worked examples verify themselves on every page load.
